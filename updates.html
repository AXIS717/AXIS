AXIS Version 1.1 Launch

August 03, 2026

Today marks an exciting milestone for AXIS as we officially launch AXIS Version 1.1.

Over the past month, Version 1.1 has been carefully redesigned, rebuilt, and refined to deliver a faster, cleaner, and more professional experience. From improved performance and enhanced responsiveness to redesigned pages and brand-new features, every update has been created with our community and future clients in mind.

Highlights of Version 1.1

• Redesigned Home page with a modern, refreshed layout.
• New Studios section showcasing our digital services.
• Fully integrated Project Quote Request system.
• Improved navigation and overall user experience.
• Expanded website content and product listings.
• Performance optimizations and bug fixes.
• Responsive improvements across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
• Enhanced SEO for better search engine visibility.
• Refined styling, animations, and visual consistency throughout the website.

Version 1.1 represents more than just a website update it reflects the passion, dedication, and countless hours invested in building a platform that truly represents the vision of AXIS.

As we continue to grow, this release lays the foundation for future features, services, and exciting developments that are already in the works.

To everyone who has supported AXIS, offered feedback, shared our work, or believed in our vision Thank You. Your support continues to inspire us to keep creating, improving, and pushing forward.

About AXIS Web Update

July 03, 2026 - August 03, 2026

Over the past month, the AXIS website has undergone one of its biggest updates yet. Our goal was simple: create a faster, cleaner, and more professional experience while laying the foundation for future features and services.

What's New?

• Completely redesigned the Home page with a modern, refreshed layout.
• Introduced the new Studios page, showcasing AXIS web design and digital services.
• Added a fully functional Project Quote Request system, making it easier for clients to start their next project.
• Expanded the website with new products and content across multiple pages.
• Fixed numerous bugs to improve stability and overall performance.
• Enhanced responsive design for a smoother experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
• Improved website navigation and user experience.
