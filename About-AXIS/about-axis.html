Designs with Direction

Designs with Direction is build upon the belief that every design should have a purpose. By combing vision, ambition, and creativity, we follow the direction of an idea and transform it into something meaningful.

WHO IS BEHIND AXIS?

AXIS was founded by 17-year-old entrepreneur and creative problem-solver Ralph-Colin Jansen van Rensburg. Starting with RC Crochet at age 12 in 2021, Ralph developed experience in design, web development, construction, business, and product creation. In 2026, he transformed those experiences into AXIS, a growing vision built around creativity, opportunity, and helping people and businesses move forward with direction.

Breaking News

On 1 June 2026, AXIS officially went live, marking the beginning of a new direction. What started as an idea became a growing vision built through creativity, ambition, and determination.

The Story

What began as RC Crochet in 2021 evolved through years of learning, creativity, and experience. In 2026, that foundation became Project AXIS, eventually growing into AXIS.

OUR MISSION

AXIS exists to help people and businesses move forward by combining creativity, problem-solving, and practical solutions. Our mission is to turn ideas into opportunities, create value, and build lasting growth with direction.

SERVICES

AXIS offers web development, creative design, product creation, community services, and business solutions. Through these services, we aim to provide practical opportunities, solve problems, and help ideas become reality.

MILESTONES

From a small creative idea to a growing business, AXIS has been shaped by every step forward. RC Crochet began in 2021, followed by Project AXIS in January 2026. On 1 June 2026, AXIS officially went live. Each milestone represents growth, learning, and the determination to keep moving forward with direction.

LATEST EDITION

AXIS is currently working on multiple projects, including building AXIS Community Services, preparing a new merchandise drop, and designing a brand-new website for a customer, while continuing to expand its creative direction.

It started with one person deciding to put his skills to work. It became a business. And the vision is for it to eventually become an ecosystem built by many talented people, each bringing their own skills, ideas, and direction. I don't know exactly where AXIS will end up.

But I know where it is going. Forward!
